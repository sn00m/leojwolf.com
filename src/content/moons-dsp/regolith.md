A Max for Live granular sample player for monome grid + arc, by moons DSP.

Regolith is based on [mangl](https://github.com/justmat/mangl) by @justmat,
which builds on angl by @tehn and glut by @artfwo. Its grain engine and
grid/arc controls are adapted from mangl and rebuilt as a Max for Live device.
Each Regolith device is one voice; add up to 4 to a Live set, one per track.
